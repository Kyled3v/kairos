# AGI Vision

## Mission

Build a KyleDev-owned artificial intelligence system capable of increasingly general problem solving across domains.

## Long-Term Capability Model

KAIROS should progress through:

1. Assistant
2. Tool-using agent
3. Multi-agent system
4. Long-horizon autonomous system
5. Adaptive learning system
6. General-purpose intelligence research platform

## Core Cognitive Loop

PERCEIVE
? UNDERSTAND
? REASON
? PLAN
? ACT
? OBSERVE
? EVALUATE
? REFLECT
? LEARN
? UPDATE MEMORY
? REASON AGAIN

## General Intelligence Areas

KAIROS will be evaluated across:

### Knowledge
Science, technology, business, humanities and other domains.

### Reasoning
Logical, causal, mathematical and multi-step reasoning.

### Planning
Short and long-horizon objective decomposition.

### Creation
Text, software, structured information and multimodal outputs.

### Perception
Text, images, documents, audio and future modalities.

### Agency
Tool selection, execution, adaptation and recovery from failure.

### Learning
Feedback integration, experience retention and strategy improvement.

### Generalization
Ability to solve unfamiliar problems without task-specific programming.

## Important Constraint

AGI is a research objective, not a guaranteed outcome.

KAIROS must report demonstrated capabilities honestly.
